The Best Movies According To Bryce

My top 3 movies



Death To Smoochy

A dark comedy written by Danny Devito, this movie explores the world of
children's entertainment as a highly corrupt, mafia run organization.
10/10... Hilarious.


Deadpool 2

The jewel in Ryan Reynold's crown, the sequel to the Deadpool franchise
doesn't have any work to do building a world or introducing characters. It
gets straight to what you want and doesn't stop delivering.


Galaxy Quest

A masterpiece of a film for anyone who grew up watching Star Trek.
